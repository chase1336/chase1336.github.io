About Me

Hi! I'm Chase Hohne, a third-year student at Boise State University majoring in Games, Interactive Media, and Mobile (GIMM). I'm passionate about creating interactive games with strong replayability, combining my technical skills in Unity and C# with my love for creating original artwork. I thrive in collaborative environments and enjoy working on team-based projects where creativity and problem-solving come together.

My Work

Galactic Buzzkill

Galactic buzzkill is a group project three of my friends and I created our freshman year. It is a time-based 2D Unity platformer that has built-in AR elements to control gravity. The objective is to solve puzzles and traverse the ship to the escape pod before it explodes.

Stuck Heading South

Stuck Heading South was the very first game I created during my time in the GIMM program. It served as a pivotal project that challenged me to apply my skills in coding, game design, art, and animation. Through this experience, I learned how to create immersive gameplay, manage project workflows, and problem solve effectively. The game was a great opportunity to showcase my ability to blend creativity with technical execution, setting the foundation for future projects.

Super Position Comic

"Super Position Comic" was a group project where we created an original concept to visually portray and teach the principles of superposition. The project aimed to make a complex quantum mechanics concept more accessible and engaging by using comic-style storytelling. Through vibrant art and simple narrative, we introduced the idea of particles existing in multiple states at once, making it easier for audiences to grasp this fundamental quantum theory. It was a fun challenge combining creativity and education in this unique format!
